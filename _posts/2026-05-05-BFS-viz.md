I'm in the process of self teaching myself Data Structures and Algorithms. The standard way to assess that is through LeetCode problems. After having tried a few myself, I can appreciate the novel solutions you have to come up with to optimize the solution. You also learn a lot about the programming language as you have to really understand how the data is flowing.

In spite of all that, I still don't like solving LeetCode problems. Most problems require you know the special trick or method to answer it. So intead of trying to solve a leetcode problem, I tried to tie it something I do like - making graphics. This is a BFS algo visualized in openframeworks. Upon pressing a key, the BFS algo steps forward and changes a uniform in the shaderso that the vertex (circle) is colored with the shader.
