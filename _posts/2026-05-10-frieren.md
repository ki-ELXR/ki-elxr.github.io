It's interesting seeing how one can use computers and algorithms to make artistic aesthetically pieces. One such idea I found from Maxime Heckel's blog was using postprocessing in a shader to give an image an almost painterly like quality to it. 

For just being a kernel that you apply to an image, the results are astounding...the other day I watched the ending of Frieren season 2, and the art style was as if it was handdrawn by pencil. I raymarched Frieren's staff from scratch and then applied the postprocessing shader to it, and found the results quite lovely.
